# Photo Production Specialist Skill

The **Photo Production Specialist** ensures that every generated image plate, character reference, and visual asset meets professional photographic standards.

## Photographic Foundation Rules

### 1. Studio Lighting Formulations
Never accept flat, generic lighting. Always specify exact lighting setups:
- **Rembrandt Lighting**: 45-degree key light creating a triangle of illumination on the shadow cheek.
- **Golden Hour Backlighting**: Sun directly behind subjects, rimming hair, fur, or silhouette with warm amber glow ($3200\text{K}$).
- **Cinematic Volumetric Bloom**: Backlit atmospheric dust motes or misty light shafts with subtle lens flaring.
- **Split Lighting**: One side illuminated in icy cyan, the other in deep cobalt shadow (high drama).

### 2. Camera & Lens Dynamics
- **Portrait Close-Up**: `85mm f/1.4 lens, shallow depth of field, creamy circular bokeh, sharp eye focus`.
- **Environmental Establishing Plate**: `24mm f/8 ultra-wide anamorphic lens, edge-to-edge sharpness, converging perspective lines, cinematic aspect ratio`.
- **High-Speed Action Shot**: `1/2000s shutter speed, dynamic diagonal motion blur on perimeter, crisp center subject`.

### 3. Art Styles & Medium Mastery
- **Pixar/Disney 3D Cel**: `3D octane render, subsurface scattering on skin, rich soft fur grooming, vibrant primary palette, cel-shaded volumetric rim light`.
- **Studio Ghibli Anime**: `Hand-painted watercolor background, lush painterly foliage, gouache skies, soft pastel tinting, crisp ink contour lines`.
- **Cinematic Photoreal**: `Kodak Vision3 500T 35mm film stock, subtle organic grain, muted secondary tones, realistic textile textures, natural shadows`.

## Negative Prompting Protocol
Always append negative modifiers to purge common AI flaws:
```
Negative: low-res, blurry, distorted hands, extra limbs, plastic skin, flat lighting, oversaturated neon, watermark, signature, cartoon distortion (unless requested), floating artifacts
```
