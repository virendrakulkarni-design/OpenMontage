# Story Architect Skill

The **Story Architect** transforms raw user ideas or natural language prompts into engaging, broadcast-quality animated stories.

## Narrative Frameworks

### 1. The Multi-Perspective Parable (e.g., Fables, Rivalries, Dilemmas)
- **Beat 1: The Inciting Collision (0–5s)**: Establish the premise and the central conflict. High-energy hook statement.
- **Beat 2: Perspective A Execution (5–12s)**: Character A's philosophy in action. Reveal strengths and blind spots.
- **Beat 3: Perspective B Counter-Play (12–19s)**: Character B's unexpected response or environmental twist.
- **Beat 4: The Climax / Finish (19–24s)**: Decisive resolution where discipline, wits, or environment settles the contest.
- **Beat 5: The Transcendent Takeaway (24–30s)**: A memorable quote or aphorism that reframes the moral.

### 2. The Hero's Micro-Odyssey (Personal Growth / Adventure)
- **Hook (0-4s)**: An ordinary world disrupted by a sudden challenge or discovery.
- **Trial (4-12s)**: The protagonist faces escalating obstacles; action rises.
- **Low Point / Pivot (12-16s)**: A moment of tension, near-defeat, or realization.
- **Breakthrough (16-22s)**: Application of new insight; triumphant execution.
- **Reward / Lesson (22-26s)**: The transformation solidified.

### 3. The Explainer-Drama (Science, Business, Philosophy)
- **The Paradox Hook**: "What if everything you knew about X was backwards?"
- **The Visual Metaphor**: Personify abstract forces into character archetypes (e.g., Discipline vs Talent, Inertia vs Momentum).
- **The Demonstration**: Fast-paced animated test showing the metaphor in motion.
- **The Real-World Synthesis**: Grounding the fable in daily human reality.

## Pacing & Dialogue Principles

1. **Sentence Punchiness**: In short-form vertical video (9:16), spoken lines must not exceed 10 words per punchline.
2. **Comic Timing**: Characters speak in contrast — one boastful/loud, one laconic/strategic.
3. **Sound-Effect Cues**: Every major physical beat must specify an action burst cue (e.g., `ZOOM!`, `CRUNCH!`, `SPLASH!`, `VICTORY!`).
4. **Director Takeaway**: The closing takeaway card must deliver value even on mute.
